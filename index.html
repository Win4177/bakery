Our Story

From a small plaza bakery to a neighborhood tradition.

Pastry House Hippo began in 1985 with a simple passion for baking and a dream: to share the unique flavors of Japanese bakeries with our community. What started as a small shop in a Mount Prospect plaza has grown into a cherished destination inside the bustling Mitsuwa Marketplace in Arlington Heights.

Today, we bake a wide range of breads and pastries that blend traditional Japanese flavors with the classics you already know and love.

“Our mission is simple: to bring joy through our baked goods — fresh every day, made with simple ingredients, with no preservatives and no artificial coloring.”
